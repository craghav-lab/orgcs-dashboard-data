CSM Outreach Plan — Structured 20-min Sync per Account
One conversation per CSM; confirm role, gaps, and ask before the next case spike
1
Identify the top 10 accounts
Pull from SFDC: case count by account, last 8 weeks, Signature tier only. Sort descending. Capture cloud breakdown, open case count, and latest CSAT.
Owner: Support Ops · Due: This week
2
Map each account to its CSM
Cross-reference against CSM assignments in CRM. Flag any accounts with no assigned CSM as a risk item — escalate to account ownership team immediately.
Owner: Support Ops · Due: This week
3
Reach out to each CSM — structured sync
Questions to cover in each conversation:
  • Are you aware of the support volume and pain points for this account?
  • What is the top issue the customer is trying to solve right now?
  • Where specifically can Industries Support add value — tactical, advisory, or escalation handling?
  • Is a Guide or Architect engagement on the radar, or already proposed?
Owner: TSE Lead / Support Manager · Due: 2 weeks
4
Capture outcomes and set a cadence
Record feedback in the tracker. For accounts with an agreed action, add a bi-weekly check-in. Surface blockers to leadership in weekly review.
Owner: Support Manager · Due: 3 weeks
↗ What "help" looks like per account pattern
  • High inflow + low CSAT → dedicated TSE time, proactive health checks
  • Recurring Sev 1s → propose Guide / Architect via CSM (see Track 2 criteria)
  • High open backlog → joint triage call: TSE + CSM + account team
  • No CSM assigned → flag to account ownership team immediately
