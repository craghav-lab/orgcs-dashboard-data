Key Insights & Recommendations
Insight 1
Industry-CPQ/OM: Structural Risk Needing Engineering Escalation
Avg age 16.2d across 24 cases; the 4 longest-running cases (50–73 days) are all blocked on Summer '26 regressions. Accounts: Telecom Argentina, SaskTel, Verizon, TDS Telecommunications. Bug W-19153042 appears to be a long-standing unresolved platform issue.

Action: Weekly sync with CPQ/OM product engineering on GUS W-22680450, W-22879106, W-23373794, W-23604856. Proactive executive communication for Verizon (52d) and SaskTel (71d).
Insight 2
Pfizer (LSC-EMEA): Needs a Single Technical DRI
Pfizer generated 18 cases in 30 days — 7.2% of team volume — across 4 different engineers. 6 are bug-linked in Managed Events. Bug W-23558456 appears across 2 separate open cases. Coordination risk and duplicate discovery effort.

Action: Designate UmaMahesh as DRI; create shared case view for the 4 covering engineers; consolidate Managed Events bugs into a product engineering bridge.
Insight 3
25.6% of Cases Are Config/Setup Errors — Self-Service Opportunity
Largest theme: customers can't activate features they purchased (PSG permissions, DocGen not in prod, OmniStudio tabs invisible, MFA setup, API Access Control). Most resolve in under 1 day.

Action: Build a "Feature Activation Checklist" for Industry Cloud, Revenue Cloud, and OmniStudio. Capturing 30% of these saves ~20 cases/month.
Insight 4
Split SLA Reporting into Engineer-Resolved vs GUS-Blocked
Bug-blocked cases average 17.8d vs 7.0d for unblocked. The 9.04d mean conflates two fundamentally different populations. Engineer performance looks inflated by cases they cannot close unilaterally.

Action: Track "time-to-GUS-filed" and "time-to-engineering-commitment" as KPIs for bug-blocked cases. Report age-excluding-bug-wait as the engineer performance metric.
Insight 5
MFA Extension Requests: Recurring Low-Value Work (14 cases, 5.6%)
13 different accounts filed MFA enforcement extension/deferral cases — nearly one per engineer per month. These are administrative policy requests, not technical problems.

Action: Explore a self-service MFA extension portal or auto-route to a non-TSE team. Proactively communicate MFA enforcement wave timelines to at-risk accounts.
